Seedless Vascular Plants

Ferns, club mosses and horsetails: the first plants to stand tall.

Classification: two lineages

🌱

Lycophytes

Club mosses. Small leaves with a single vein. Ancestors were giant trees.

🌿

Monilophytes

Ferns and horsetails. Ferns have large divided fronds; horsetails have jointed stems.
